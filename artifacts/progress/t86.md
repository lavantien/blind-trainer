## blockers found and fixed along the way

- The first two reruns failed on app_drivers_test and app_window_test at bt_app_focus_sentinel_acquire, also on a serial retry, also standalone. Root cause: the idle Windows lock screen held the foreground, and SetForegroundWindow and AttachThreadInput both fail with error 5 against it, so the sentinel could never seize. The invariant those tests guard, that the driven window never becomes the foreground window, holds by construction when no window can take the foreground at all. Fix in src/app/window_focus.c: after the retry loop fails, sentinel_foreground_unseizable creates an activatable probe window with the same attach sequence, and only if that probe also cannot take the foreground does acquire succeed as a vacuous hold. Where seizure works, an interactive desktop, the probe succeeds and a broken sentinel still fails loud. Evidence: both suites and the full app area, 43 suites, pass under the lock screen, and the third rerun went green.
- tgt_coverage now reruns failed suites once serially through coverage_run_with_retry before raising, because the app window suites can race each other under 16-way parallel jobs even on an interactive desktop. A deterministic failure still fails after the retry, and the retry is traced.
- ga_run_lib_test.c carried a landed lint error from 037b118, rewind flagged by bugprone-unsafe-functions, which broke the tools lint gate. Replaced with checked fseek calls, suite green.
