## Frozen interface notes for the hub

Raised in the lane report: the state table draw rows cannot reach the
loaded fonts, window.c holds them in statics with no getter, so a
bt_app_fonts_current accessor in window.h is required before any view
draw row goes live. images.rs (the 620-entry image word table) belongs
to L18 by the plan but memo and drill_card consume it, ruling needed.
