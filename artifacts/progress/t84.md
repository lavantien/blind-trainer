## atlas recommendation for the hub (window.c LoadFontEx base, line 194 after the t83 landing, lane-83 file)

LoadFontEx base 64 -> 84. Derivation: the atlas base must be at least the largest size any role draws so DrawTextEx never upscales; the final ladder maximum is font_focal x root_rem = 3.0 x 28 = 84.0 px exactly, so 84 is the smallest base that removes the focal upscale (1.49x on the old 95.2 px focal, still 1.31x on the new 84 px focal if 64 is kept). Every smaller role then minifies: title 0.67, body 0.33, small 0.32, micro 0.30 of the atlas.

Caveat from raylib 6.2 source: rtext.c line 377 sets TEXTURE_FILTER_POINT on font textures, and point filtering drops pixels on minification, so minified roles show stroke dropout at any base well above their draw size. If the hub takes the 84 base, also consider SetTextureFilter BILINEAR on both faces in the same touch. After any atlas change, remeasure the subtext bands with playground/t84: the 19 to 20 px hint bands below were measured at base 64.

## subtext floor closure

The strict "every visible subtext band at least 20 px" cannot hold under the fixed 28 px body. Ink extents by glyph class at nominal N: ascender-plus-descender strings 0.92N, digit or cap strings 0.71N, x-height-only strings 0.52N, so 20 px bands need nominal 21.7, 28.2, and 38.5 px respectively and the last two breach the body. The shipped ladder puts nominal subtext at 25.2 and 26.88 px (the 24 px nominal target is met) and lifts the named case, the settings hint lines, from 16 px to 19-20 px measured. Bands that stay under 20 px: algs "818 of 818 cases match" 14, menu data row3 14, header context 11, one settings hint 19.
